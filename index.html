MOMBASA ROAD - MAKINDU KENYA

Exactly the point where the journey rests.

A stopover lodge, restaurant,confrence hall and Gym built for the road between Nairobi and Mombasa - three hundred kilometres either way, and Makindu sits right in the middle.

WHY TRAVELLERS STOP HERE
A working town not a truck stop.
Makindu has fed and rested travellers on the Nairobi- Mombasa road for generations - long before it was tarmac. Makindu restaurant keeps that role: clean rooms for the night, a kitchen that Runs on kenyan staples, a hall for the meeting you couldn't reschedule , and a gym for the drivers , delegates and families who'd rather not lose the routine just because they're on the road.
